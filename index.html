Experience Beautiful Baku

Located in the heart of this stunning city, Asian King Restaurant perfectly blends the rich, ancient history of Baku with exquisite Asian culinary artistry. Enjoy your meal, then explore the magic of the Old City and the modern glow of the Flame Towers.

Caspian Sea Boulevard

Enjoy a refreshing walk along the beautiful Milli Park before indulging in our exquisite Asian cuisine.

Modern Masterpiece

A true architectural wonder representing the modern spirit of Baku. A must-see on your culinary journey.

Modern Elegance

Just steps away from the iconic Flame Towers and the beautiful Caspian Sea promenade. A perfect view after an unforgettable dining experience.

Ancient Charm

Nestled near the historic cobblestone streets of Icherisheher. Take a romantic evening stroll through the glowing, magical old town.
